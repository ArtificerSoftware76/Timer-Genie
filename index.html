Common questions

How do I edit or delete a workout?

On the My Workouts screen, swipe a workout to the right and tap Edit. Swipe to the left and tap Delete to remove it. Press and hold a workout to duplicate it.

Will the bell stop my music?

No. Timer Genie's sounds play over your music without pausing it or turning it down. Use the Timer volume slider in Settings to balance them.

What happens when I get a phone call?

The timer keeps running silently during calls. If you'd rather it pause, turn on "Pause timer during phone calls" in Settings. It only pauses when you answer.

Can I change settings during a workout?

Yes. Tap the gear in the top-right corner of the timer screen.
