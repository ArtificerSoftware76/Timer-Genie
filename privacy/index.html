Timer Genie Privacy Policy

Awakened Fitness, LLC · Effective November 3, 2026

In short: Timer Genie does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you set up in the app stays on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. Timer Genie does not send any information to us or to anyone else. It has no sign-in, does not connect to our servers and does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Information stored on your iPhone

To work, the app saves a few things on your iPhone only:

  • The workouts you create (names, times, sets, rounds and sound choices).
  • Your settings, such as the timer volume and whether to pause the timer during phone calls.

We never see this information. It is deleted when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, it may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under your own backup settings.

Phone calls

Timer Genie checks whether a phone call is in progress so it can stay silent during calls and, if you turn on the setting, pause your workout when you answer. It uses Apple's standard call status feature for this. The app cannot see who is calling, phone numbers or anything said on the call, and it does not record or save anything about your calls.

Microphone, location, contacts and health data

Timer Genie does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or health data.

Children

Timer Genie does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
